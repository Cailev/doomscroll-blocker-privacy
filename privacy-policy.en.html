Privacy policy for Pagend: Page Blocker

Deutsche Fassung

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Controller

Luca Schick, email: dev@cailev.com. This policy applies to the Pagend: Page Blocker browser extension in Chrome, Opera, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Firefox, and to this page itself.

In short

  • Pagend processes usage data locally in your browser to provide its features. I do not receive usage history, settings or reflections from the extension.
  • Everything the extension stores stays on your device, in the extension’s storage in your browser.
  • There is no server, no account, no advertising, no analytics tool and no sync.
  • Extension pages are restricted from making network requests (connect-src 'none'). External links open the relevant website in your browser.
  • Data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties, because it never leaves the device.
  • If you save a backup, a table or a text file, it stays wherever you save it. Automatic backup only writes if you turn it on.

Storing on your device

The extension stores on your device only what is strictly necessary for the feature you asked for (standard: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, the German telecommunications and digital services data protection act). There are no cookies and no identifiers that could recognise you. The table shows what is stored and for how long.

Web activity

Only sites on your list are recorded. Only the site itself is stored permanently, never an exact address, no path of a visited address and no page title, and only daily totals, not when exactly you were on a site. The extension only stores a path if you create it yourself as a section (“Only this section”), and then only that path, never query (?…) or anchor (#…). Through the webNavigation permission the extension learns about page visits on all sites, but only compares the address with your list. If the site isn’t on it, nothing happens and nothing is stored. It doesn’t read your saved browsing history.

A small script runs on sites you turned on. It sends the page address and whether the page is visible to the extension itself and nowhere else. It doesn’t read page content.

Limit this site

You can limit a site from the popup at the icon, by right-clicking a web page (contextMenus permission) and in Firefox also by right-clicking the tab. The extension only reads the page address after your click (activeTab or the click in the menu) and keeps only the domain and a path suggestion, in working memory only. You choose “Whole site” or “Only this section”. Nothing is stored until you actually add it: the domain, and with “Only this section” the path as an own section.

Automatic backup

If you turn on automatic backup under “General”, the browser asks for the downloads permission. The extension then saves a backup daily, hourly or at your interval into a subfolder of your downloads folder. It only runs while the browser is running, and the file isn’t sent anywhere.

  • Reflections are only included if you turn on “Include reflections”. The default is without.
  • Only the newest own files stay in the subfolder (default 7, adjustable). The extension removes older ones along with their entry in the browser’s download history. For this it only keeps the download IDs of its own files. It can’t reach files you moved or renamed.
  • Every backup shows up in the browser’s download history until the rotation removes it.
  • When you turn it off, the extension asks whether the existing files should stay.
  • Without permission or if saving fails, the extension keeps the last 2 backups itself. These are lost with the browser profile and can be saved as a file under “General”. Deleting and deletion periods apply to them too.

Storage, updates and messages

The extension uses the unlimitedStorage permission. It removes the 10 MB limit of local storage so old reflections and days don’t have to go. Your data stays on your device, and nothing additional is recorded because of it.

Before a change of the data version during an update, the extension makes a copy of the affected settings, without history and without reflections (migrationBackup). It is restored if something goes wrong, goes away 3 days after the update and is removed by “Delete absolutely everything”.

If the drive is full anyway, the extension removes the oldest reflections first, then the oldest days, and tells you on its pages. Only the number is kept for that. A backup copy never pushes out your data. Problems while saving are remembered only as the kind of problem, without any content, and only until the browser closes (storageIssues).

Reflections

Reflections can be very personal, for example about mood or health. They only live on your device, can be turned off, read and deleted under “Privacy”, and only go into a backup if you want them to. The provider never receives them.

Voluntary, no automated decisions

You don’t have to provide any data. Everything is voluntary, the extension also works without reflections and without history. There are no automated decisions about you and no profiling: the block only follows your own rules.

Children

The extension doesn’t record any age information, and no data goes to the provider.

The detected browser

When showing a page, the extension detects which browser is running so the right texts appear. The result is never stored and never sent.

Firefox

The Firefox package is built from the same source code and processes the same data in the same way. In the package, Mozilla is told that the extension collects no data (data_collection_permissions: none).

Warnings as system notifications

If you turn on the warning as a system notification, the browser asks for the notifications permission. The notification is created on your device and shown by the operating system. It isn’t sent anywhere.

Contact on the “Info” page

“Info” shows the address for feature requests and bugs as an email link. A click opens your own email app. The extension doesn’t connect anywhere and sends nothing along, no data and no version.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). The extension’s data only lives on your device, so you can exercise these rights yourself: under “Privacy” you see what is stored, read your reflections and delete specific items or everything. Under “General”, “Save backup” puts everything into a file you can take with you. For emails and any questions, contact dev@cailev.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example in the country where you live (Art. 77 GDPR).

How to delete data

  • Under “Privacy” you decide what goes into the history and delete by kind, period and site, or everything.
  • Automatic deletion periods under “Privacy”: separately per kind (time used, statistics, reflections) and as a shared period “Delete all data”, each Never, 3, 6 or 12 months or your own number of calendar days or calendar months. Once a day, anything older goes. Settings, achievements and today stay.
  • Targeted deletion and the deletion periods also apply to the fallback copies of the automatic backup kept with the extension.
  • “Delete absolutely everything” removes all data right away. Only settings and the site list as in a new installation, your privacy switches (so that recording you turned off stays off) and technical minimum values for measuring and blocking remain. The fallback copies and, as far as reachable, the automatic backup’s own files in the downloads folder are removed too.
  • The “Your time and statistics” feature can be turned off under “General”. Then nothing new goes into the history.
  • When you remove the extension, the browser deletes its storage. Exported files, saved backups and the browser’s own history stay. Without a saved backup the data can’t be rescued.

Limits

The data isn’t encrypted. Anyone who can use your browser profile can read it, reflections too.

A backup file isn’t encrypted and contains your site list, your history and, if you want, your texts. There is no sync and no protection against files changed by hand.
