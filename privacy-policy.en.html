Doomscroll Blocker privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026. Provider: Luca Schick. Contact: dev@cailev.com.

In short

  • Everything stays on your device, in the extension’s storage in your browser.
  • There is no server, no account, no advertising, no analytics tool and no sync.
  • The extension’s pages technically cannot open connections to the internet (Content Security Policy with connect-src 'none').
  • Data is neither sold nor passed on to third parties, because it does not leave the device.

Web activity

Only sites on your list are recorded. Only the site is stored permanently, never an exact address, path or page title, and only daily totals, so not when exactly you were on a site. Through the webNavigation permission the extension learns about visits to all sites, but it only compares the address with your list. If the site is not on it, nothing happens and nothing is stored. It does not read your saved browsing history.

A small script runs on sites you turned on. It sends the site’s address to the extension itself and nowhere else. It does not read any content of the page.

The detected browser

When drawing its pages, the extension detects which browser is running so that the right texts appear. The result is never saved and never sent.

How to delete data

  • Under “Privacy” you decide what goes into the history and delete selectively or everything.
  • “Delete everything” removes all data right away. With Cool-off, the settings follow the next day.
  • The statistics feature can be turned off under “General”. Then nothing new gets into the history.
  • When you remove the extension, the browser deletes its storage. Exported text files and the browser’s own history stay.

Limits

The data is not encrypted. Anyone who can use your browser profile can read it, including reflection texts.

Permissions
